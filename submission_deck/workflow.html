Core product flow

One flow turns papers into review decisions

The workflow creates a clear hand-off between document intake, extraction context, evidence mapping, and teacher judgment.

1

Upload

Teacher selects one question paper and one handwritten answer sheet.

2

Extracting

A dedicated transition signals that the paper is being prepared for review.

3

Map evidence

Selecting a question moves the exact answer-sheet highlight into view.

4

Review & save

Teacher decisions become a durable record available from My Library.

Teacher decision point: answer regions support review; they do not replace the teacher’s judgment.
